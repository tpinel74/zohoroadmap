Zoho CRM Roadmap

Where we are and what comes next as Membership Advocates move from Engage Pro to Zoho. There are no firm dates. Each item moves when it is resolved, and the page is refreshed a couple of times a week.

How a lead moves

Lead arrives
Website forms, Nimbata calls, chat
New Lead Queue
Owner is Membership Team. The club shows on each lead.
Claimed
An advocate takes it. Owner becomes that advocate and the cadence signs as them.
Contacted
The cadence stops. The advocate nurtures from here.
Qualified
Criteria still being defined. Converts to Contact, Account and Deal.
Deal
Blueprint stages through to Closed Won or Closed Lost.

Walk-ins, phone inquiries and advocate referrals skip the queue. The advocate owns them from the moment they are entered, so they need no cadence. Blue steps are where an advocate is working.
